Frequently Asked Questions

What types of projects do you work on?

+

I work on characters, environments, product visualization, and game assets.

What is your turnaround time?

+

Depending on project size, usually between 3 days to 2 weeks.

Do you work long-term?

+

Yes, I'm open to long-term freelance collaborations.

Can you create game-ready assets?

+

Yes. I can prepare clean, optimized models with sensible topology, UVs, baked maps, and PBR textures.

Which software do you use?

+

I work with Maya, Blender, ZBrush, Substance Painter, Photoshop, and Marvelous Designer depending on the project.

Can you match a reference style?

+

Yes. Share references, mood boards, or sketches and I can match realistic, stylized, cinematic, or game-art directions.

Do you provide progress updates?

+

Yes. I usually share blockout, modeling, texturing, lighting, and final render stages so feedback is easy and timely.

What files can you deliver?

+

I can deliver renders and common 3D formats such as FBX, OBJ, blend, Maya files, texture maps, and presentation images.

How do we start a project?

+

Send the idea, references, expected output, deadline, and budget range. I will reply with a clear scope and next steps.
